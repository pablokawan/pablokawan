## Hi there, I'm Pablo Kawan 👋
I am CTO and partner at [Serveloja][serveloja], a payment company operating as a subacquirer since 2009, exempt from authorization by the Banco Central do Brasil pursuant to Circular nº 3.885/2018, while fully complying with applicable regulations, including Circulars nº 3.682/2013, 3.886/2018, 3.952/2019 and Resolution nº 24/2020. We operate as an participant under ISPB [10773370][ispb] and participate in the [MASTERCARD][mastercard], [AMEX][amex], [VISA][visa], [ELO][elo], [DINERS](about:blank), [CABAL](about:blank), [DISCOVER](about:blank), [AURA](about:blank) and [JCB](about:blank) card arrangements. We offer tailored payment, [liquidation services][cip], all backed by PCI-DSS certification and in-house technology. Our mission is to facilitate the growth of small business by providing simplified access to the latest financial technologies.
